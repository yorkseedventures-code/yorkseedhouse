What Is Yorkseed House

A space built for signal, not noise.

Yorkseed House is our recurring pop-up activation — a physical gathering space that travels with the world's most important tech and innovation events.

Each edition is independently produced with a curated partner network, a distinct environment, and programming designed for real conversations. Not panels for the sake of panels. Not networking for the sake of business cards.

Every house has its own identity. The same commitment to quality.
