About the House

Built in 24 hours.
Remembered longer.

In true startup fashion, Yorkseed House at SXSW 2025 came together in less than 24 hours — and it delivered. Hosted at The Lively & Grit Daily House in Austin, it became one of the most talked-about activations of the week.

Founders, investors, operators, and ecosystem leaders spent a full day connecting, pitching, learning, and building relationships that extended well beyond the conference. The house ran from 9:30AM to 5PM with nonstop programming — a true home base at SXSW.

The centrepiece was the Yorkseed Investor Panel: Beyond the Check — a candid, high-energy discussion with six top investors on what makes founders truly investable.

Investor Panel

Beyond The Check

What investors look for beyond capital returns — and how to win over an investor in 5 minutes. A candid, no-fluff panel with six top investors sharing unfiltered insights on what makes founders truly investable.
