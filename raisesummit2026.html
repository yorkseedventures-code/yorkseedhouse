As founders, investors, executives, and ecosystem leaders gather for RAISE Summit, Yorkseed is opening the doors to Yorkseed House — a curated venture headquarters designed to bring the global innovation ecosystem together beyond the conference floor.

More than an event venue — a place to work, meet, recharge, collaborate, and build meaningful relationships throughout the week. Drop in. Drop out. Come back anytime.

Complimentary · Advance Registration Required

Yorkseed House is proudly hosted at the Paris office of OBWB (Osha Bergman Watanabe & Burton) — a leading international intellectual property law firm advising startups, scaleups, and investors on IP, AI, patents, trademarks, and innovation strategy.

Located at 2 Rue de la Paix, steps from the Louvre and RAISE Summit — one of the most prestigious addresses in Paris. We are grateful to Jonathan and the entire OBWB team for opening their doors.
